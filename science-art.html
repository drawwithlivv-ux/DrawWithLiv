Skull - Right View

Science Behind the Artwork

The first labelled image shows the labelled osteology (bone structures) of the skeleton! This was done to show where each bone resides in the skull and to help me memorise how the skull is arranged, this helps with memorizing where different nerves or arteries may reside since i am now familiar with the general structure of the skull so i can now use that to help me visualise where the nerves or veins may be using spatial relationships! The second will show the facial nerves and where they reside on the skull - some of these would be inside the skull and loop over rather being exactly how i have drawn it but they are in the correct places - just not drawn in each view if that makes sense!
