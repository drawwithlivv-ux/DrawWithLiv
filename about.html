🌿 About the Artist 🌿

The person behind DrawWithLivv

Hi, I'm Livv! 🌸

I'm an artist inspired by curiosity, nature and imagination. Alongside creating artwork, I'm studying Biomedical Science with a particular interest in neuroscience. Although science and art may seem very different, both allow me to explore the world through creativity, observation and attention to detail. I hope my artwork brings a little warmth and happiness to everyone who visits.

🍃 What Inspires Me 🍃

🐾 Wildlife 🐾

Animals and their unique personalities inspire many of my illustrations.

🌿 Nature 🌿

Soft colours, forests and peaceful scenery influence my style.

✨ Imagination ✨

Fantasy creatures and original ideas let me explore creativity without limits.

🦠 Science 🦠

Every aspect of life has the involvement of the scientific processes i enjoy learning about, each process has such remarkable fundamentals and structures, these inspire me to create art in many different shapes, such as spirals - as inspired by my favourite cyanobacterium Spirulina (Limnospira platensis).

🌸 Thank You

Thank you for taking the time to visit my little corner of the internet. I hope you enjoy my artwork as much as I enjoy creating it. 💚
