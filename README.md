🎨 About

DrawWithLivv is a personal art gallery and portfolio featuring my artwork, with a focus on traditional pieces alongside some of my newer digital work.

The website also includes information about commissions, how to get in touch with me and my social medias where i promote my website!

💚 About the Project

This website was created as a personal project to build an online portfolio for my artwork and to learn more about web development along the way.

I'm continuing to update the website as I add new artwork and improve its design and functionality. I have recently added a study relevant section which I aim to create proper revision tools out of in the future, as I grow and expand my website I hope to support many people with both studying but also with putting ideas to paper! I am also experimenting with 3D design and animation at the moment, I hope to incorporate some of this into my website very soon.
