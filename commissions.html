💌 My Process

1️⃣ Email me your idea.

2️⃣ We'll discuss the details together.

3️⃣ I'll create an initial sketch.

4️⃣ Minor revisions can be made if needed.

5️⃣ I'll complete the artwork and send you the finished piece.

🍃 🌸 🍃

📝 Before You Email Me

Please include as much information as possible:

🐾 What you'd like me to draw

📷 Any reference images

🎨 Colours, themes or ideas

📐 Preferred canvas size (if you have one)

✨ Anything else you'd like me to know!

💳 Payment & Pricing

💳 Payment

Payments are currently accepted through PayPal.

Payment will be done in two halfs, the first upon revision of the initial sketch and the second upon completion. When it comes to payment I will send you the link to my paypal account and you can pay me through that, or if you don't have paypal we can discuss other options.

💷 Pricing

Initial pricing starts at ~£50 GBP.

Every commission is unique.

Please contact me for a personalised quote.
