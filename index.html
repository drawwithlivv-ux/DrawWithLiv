🌿 About Me

Hi! I'm Livv, an artist driven by curiosity and creativity.

Whether I'm studying neuroscience or bringing my imagination to life through art, I love discovering how things work and watching paintings come to life one brushstroke at a time.

I hope you enjoy my artwork! If you'd like to commission a piece, I'd love to hear from you through my email or social media.

🌸 Sneak peak at my Gallery 🌸

Welcome to my little collection of artwork!

Here you'll find illustrations inspired by animals, nature and imagination. Every piece is created with care, and I hope they bring a little warmth and joy.

While I've spent many years creating traditional artwork, I've only recently begun my journey into digital illustration. This gallery will continue to grow as I complete new pieces, and I'm excited to share that journey with you.

Lil' Duckie

Curious Tiger

A work in progress, motivated by my interest in the "unsettling stare" of a tiger as it listens to and watches its prey. I plan on bringing this piece to life with my knowledge of temperature and dynamics when using colour.
